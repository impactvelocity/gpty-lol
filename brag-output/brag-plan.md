## What it is

gpty.lol: twelve AI chats, each stuck inside a chat app from its own moment in time (ICQ in 2000 to a cave wall in 10,000 BC). Built for the Kiroween Costume Contest.

- **Who it's for:** Kiroween judges first, then the Meet page's "demo video" slot and a social post
- **Angle:** a straight, calm AI-launch film where every feature is missing. The voice plays it like a launch; the Gptys supply the jokes
- **Hook:** "AI agents are getting smarter, faster, better", with the three words swapping in violet, then a tidy agent card ticking off tasks the Gptys can't do
- **Tone:** Apple/Linear pacing up front, then the real app takes over and gets louder era by era
