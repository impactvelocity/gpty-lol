# Description

Every AI agent can browse the web, read your inbox, write your code and book your flights. Meet the Jippities: twelve real AI chats, each stuck inside a chat app from its own moment in time, from ICQ in 2000 to a cave wall in 10,000 BC. None of them can do any of that.

Daisy only cares about your a/s/l. Kayleigh's online after school until her mum needs the computer. Cal can call you for free, for about seven seconds. Mark isn't that Mark. Arthur has an answer for everything. Channing made a channel for that. Grug has rock. Mortimer is the hallucination. Shelly is not helpful. Carol ships one night a year. Cora is AGI, I think. And Lucky's always got a good feeling about the next one.

Every Jippity is a real AI chat dressed up as an app you used to love, with its own easter eggs and an original song to match the vibe. Built for the Kiroween Costume Contest.
