# Gpty: AI agents that can't do anything | Kiroween Costume Contest, Kiro

## Description

Twelve real AI chats, each stuck inside a chat app from its own moment in time. Daisy only cares about your a/s/l on ICQ. Kayleigh's on MSN until her mum needs the computer. Cal's Skype call lasts about seven seconds. Grug has a rock. Every one has its own window, its own easter eggs and its own theme song, and none of them can browse the web, read your inbox or book your flights.

Built for the Kiroween Costume Contest with the Kiro IDE.
