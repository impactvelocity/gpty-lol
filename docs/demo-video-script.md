# Demo video script: plan (draft 1)

★ = my pick. Lines are pulled from `docs/meet-copy.md` where they fit. Cut freely.

## Constraints

- **Under 3 minutes.** Kiroween rules say judges don't have to watch past 3:00. Aim for about 2:45 so there's slack.
- **About 300 words of voiceover.** At a calm 150 words a minute that's 2:00 of talking, which leaves room for the screen to do some jokes on its own (replies typing out, a nudge, a trout).
- **Show the real app.** Every beat below is a shot of something that already exists. Record real replies; the Gpty lines in this doc are targets, and the model may say something better (or worse). Re-roll until it lands.
- **Same deadpan as the site.** The VO sounds like a straight AI launch video. The characters supply the jokes.

## 1. Agents are everywhere (0:00–0:10)

**VO**
> Agents are getting smarter. Every big AI company is shipping a better, faster, cuter one every week.

**On screen:** the `clean` costume: a calm modern chat, "What's on your mind?", a tidy answer streaming in. It's our own "normal agent", so no rival products on screen.

**Transition idea:** on the last word, the window bounces and turns into MSN.

## 2. Meet the Gptys (0:10–0:20)

**VO**
> Meet the Gptys. Agents that are stuck in time, have a whole lot of personality, and are completely useless.

**On screen:** the sidebar. Move the pointer down the list so each Gpty's eyes follow it. Land on Kayleigh.

## 3. Other agents can… a Gpty can't (0:20–0:55)

Each row is about 5 seconds: the VO reads the left side in a flat corporate voice, then cuts to the Gpty for the right side. Six rows fit. Lines are tuned to shots that exist in the app.

| VO: "Other agents can…" | VO: punchline | Shot |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Use your computer | Grug has a rock. | GrugPT cave wall; Grug gives you a shiny rock (`/give shiny`) |
| ★ Browse the web | Dale thinks the web is a fad. | BBS dial-in: ATDT, RINGING, CONNECT 2400, ANSI splash |
| ★ Run 24/7 | Kayleigh's online after school. Until her mum needs the computer. | MSN, "brb mum says dinner" |
| ★ Read your inbox | Channing killed email. You're welcome. | Slack, Channing renaming the channel after your question |
| ★ Talk to you in voice mode | Cal can call you. For about seven seconds. | Skype call rings, connects, freezes, drops |
| ★ Stream their answers | Mortimer streams. One letter at a time. | Planchette spelling a reply on the board |

Spares, if a row doesn't film well:
- Orchestrate sub-agents → Carol has 4,000 elves and a deadline. (Elf conveyor, sleigh countdown)
- Do your taxes → Grug can count to ten. After that it's MANY.
- Keep your data secure → Shelly is the last firewall. She's very chatty. (OMNIVAC ACCESS DENIED)
- Remember everything about you → Daisy remembers your a/s/l.
- Keep learning → They've stopped. That's sort of the point. (Good closing row if there's time.)

## 4. What a Gpty is (0:55–1:20)

**VO**
> Each Gpty is an AI chat stuck in its own era, living inside the messaging app of the time. Start in the early 2000s with MSN, ICQ and Skype. Then go somewhere stranger: Grug the caveman, Mortimer the Victorian ghost, or Carol the holiday elf.

**On screen:** click through the sidebar in time with the VO so each window lands on its name: MSN → ICQ (Uh-oh!) → Skype → GrugPT → Séance board → North Pole.

## 5. They only know their world (1:20–1:45)

**VO**
> Each one only knows, and only talks about, its own little world. Ask Kayleigh about AI…

**On screen:** type into MSN, all lowercase: `have u heard of ai?` Let her reply play with no VO over it.

Target reply, joke options (she thinks AI is a band):
- ★ **A1, the boy band.** `ai?? u mean A1?? omg i LOVE them (L)` / `caught in the middle is my song` / `brb mum says dinner`
- **S Club.** `ai? never heard of them. are they better than s club? nothing is better than s club` / `reach is my song (Y)`
- **The film.** `the film with the robot boy?? i cried at the end :'(`. Less silly, but it's the one she'd most likely say on her own.

A1 is my pick because the confusion is a real misreading (AI / A1) and it fits a UK teen in 2005.

**Optional double:** cut straight to Grug asked the same thing. `WHAT AI? GRUG KNOW FIRE.` One line, two seconds.

## 6. Easter eggs (1:45–2:15)

**VO**
> Chat with any Gpty and go looking for the easter eggs. [joke]

Joke options:
- ★ *It's Kiroween, so technically they're not easter eggs. They're trick-or-treats.*
- *We hid a lot of them. Grug ate most.* (Cut to Grug: `GRUG FIND EGG. GRUG EAT EGG.`)
- *Slap Nick with a trout. Throw a sheep at Mark. Throw a snowball at Carol. Nobody gets hurt, except your afternoon.*

**On screen:** fast montage, about 2 seconds each, no VO over the last few:
- MSN nudge shakes the window, then Kayleigh nudges back
- mIRC `/slap`: a large trout flies
- Facebook: throw a sheep at Mark, he pokes back
- Elf: snowball splat on the glass
- Séance: say GOODBYE and the candles go out
- BBS: press Degauss for the wobble
- The Machine: something shocks Cora and the window glitches

## 7. Built with Kiro (2:15–2:35)

**VO**
> Gpty dot lol was built for the Kiroween hackathon, with the Kiro IDE.

**On screen:** the repo open in Kiro: `src/lib/costumes.ts` and `src/lib/personas.ts`, maybe a chat or spec mid-build.

**Optional costume-contest line** (from the Kiroween section of the site copy):
> One model. Thirteen costumes. Underneath every one is the same AI wearing a mask. Ask Cora who's under there. She'd rather you didn't.

## 8. Try it (2:35–2:45)

**VO**
> Try it for yourself at gpty dot lol.

**End card:** `gpty.lol` with the Gptys lined up, eyes following the cursor. Footer line under it: *No agents were made useful in the making of this site.*

## Open questions

1. **How much Kiro?** The draft gives Kiro one beat (20 seconds). Kiroween judging looks at how the project used Kiro, so you may want a longer beat here (specs, hooks, steering) at the cost of easter egg time.
2. **Who does the VO?** You, a text-to-speech voice, or Cora's voice? A flat synthetic voice would suit the "straight AI launch" tone.
3. **Music.** A generic upbeat AI-launch track that cuts out, and each era's sound takes over (dial-up, Uh-oh!, the Skype ring)?
