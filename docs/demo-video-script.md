# Demo video script: plan (draft 2)

★ = my pick. Lines are pulled from `docs/meet-copy.md` and the Meet page where they fit. Cut freely.

Draft 2 only uses the twelve Gptys in the sidebar. Dale (BBS), Nick (mIRC), the 1989 terminal Shelly and the high-limit Lucky are hidden, so they're out of the video.

## Constraints

- **Under 3 minutes.** Kiroween rules say judges don't have to watch past 3:00. Aim for about 2:45 so there's slack.
- **About 300 words of voiceover.** At a calm 150 words a minute that's 2:00 of talking, which leaves room for the screen to do some jokes on its own (replies typing out, a nudge, a near miss).
- **Show the real app.** Every beat below is a shot of something that already exists. Record real replies; the Gpty lines in this doc are targets, and the model may say something better (or worse). Re-roll until it lands.
- **Same deadpan as the site.** The VO sounds like a straight AI launch video. The characters supply the jokes.

## 1. Agents are everywhere (0:00–0:10)

**VO**
> Agents are getting smarter. Every big AI company is shipping a better, faster, cuter one every week.

**On screen:** `gpty.lol/clean`: a calm modern chat, "Message gpty…", a tidy answer streaming in. It's our own "normal agent", so no rival products on screen.

**Transition idea:** on the last word, the window bounces and turns into MSN.

## 2. Meet the Gptys (0:10–0:20)

**VO**
> Meet the Gptys. Agents that are stuck in time, have a whole lot of personality, and are completely useless.

**On screen:** the sidebar. Move the pointer down the list so each Gpty's eyes follow it. Land on Kayleigh.

## 3. Other agents can… a Gpty can't (0:20–0:55)

Each row is about 5 seconds: the VO reads the left side in a flat corporate voice, then cuts to the Gpty for the right side. Six rows fit. Every shot below is a built feature, so none of them depend on the model saying the right thing.

| VO: "Other agents can…" | VO: punchline | Shot |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Use your computer | Grug has a rock. | Cave wall; give Grug a shiny rock from the + menu, he gives you something back |
| ★ Read your inbox | Channing killed email. You're welcome. | Slack: ask a question in #yo-channing and Channing renames the channel after it |
| ★ Talk to you in voice mode | Cal can call you. For about seven seconds. | Skype: the green Call button; it rings, connects, freezes, drops |
| ★ Stream their answers | Mortimer streams. One letter at a time. | The planchette spelling a reply on the board |
| ★ Write and run code | Shelly is a firewall. Against you. | Type HACK: HACK.EXE runs one character at a time, then BLOCKED |
| ★ Get it right first time | Lucky's next answer is the one. He can feel it. | Pull the lever: two of a kind, the third reel stops one past the line, PULL AGAIN |

Spares, if a row doesn't film well:
- Run 24/7 → Kayleigh logs off for tea. (Depends on the model; re-roll until she says brb.)
- Cite their sources → Arthur links an xkcd. (Also model-dependent.)
- Browse the web → Mark is on Facebook. That's the web. (The 2008 Firefox with the YouTube tab he forgot about.)
- Remember everything about you → Daisy remembers your a/s/l.
- Orchestrate sub-agents → Carol has 4,000 elves and a deadline. (Her greeting: 4,000 toy trucks behind.)
- Speak your language → Cora speaks machine. (Your own words previewed in machinespeak above the input.)
- Do your taxes → Grug can count to ten. After that it's MANY.
- Keep learning → They've stopped. That's sort of the point. (Good closing row if there's time.)

## 4. What a Gpty is (0:55–1:20)

**VO**
> Each Gpty is an AI chat stuck in its own era, living inside the messaging app of the time. ICQ in 2000, MSN, Skype, Facebook, reddit, all the way to Slack. Then it gets stranger: Grug the caveman, Mortimer the Victorian ghost, Carol the holiday elf, and Lucky, a slot machine at 3 a.m.

**On screen:** click down the sidebar in time with the VO so each window lands on its name: ICQ (Uh-oh!) → MSN → Skype → Facebook → reddit → Slack, faster as it goes, then slow down for Grug → Mortimer → Carol → Lucky.

Shelly and Cora aren't named here to keep the VO short. They get their moment in beat 6 and the optional line in beat 7.

## 5. They only know their world (1:20–1:45)

**VO**
> Each one only knows, and only talks about, its own little world. Ask Kayleigh about AI…

**On screen:** type into MSN, all lowercase: `have u heard of ai?` Let her reply play with no VO over it.

Target reply, joke options (she thinks AI is a band):
- ★ **A1, the boy band.** `ai?? u mean A1?? omg i LOVE them (L)` / `caught in the middle is my song` / `brb mum says dinner`
- **S Club.** `ai? never heard of them. are they better than s club? nothing is better than s club` / `reach is my song (Y)`
- **The film.** `the film with the robot boy?? i cried at the end :'(`. Less silly, but it's the one she'd most likely say on her own.

A1 is my pick because the confusion is a real misreading (AI / A1) and it fits a UK teen in 2005.

**Optional double:** cut straight to Grug asked the same thing. `WHAT AI? GRUG KNOW FIRE.` One line, two seconds.

## 6. Easter eggs (1:45–2:15)

**VO**
> Chat with any Gpty and go looking for the easter eggs. [joke]

Joke options:
- ★ *It's Kiroween, so technically they're not easter eggs. They're trick-or-treats.*
- *We hid a lot of them. Grug ate most.* (Cut to Grug: `GRUG FIND EGG. GRUG EAT EGG.`)
- *Throw a sheep at Mark. Throw a snowball at Carol. Kick Shelly. Nobody gets hurt, except your afternoon.*

**On screen:** fast montage, about 2 seconds each, no VO over the last few:
- MSN: Nudge shakes the window, then Kayleigh nudges back
- Facebook: throw a sheep at Mark, he pokes back
- Elf: throw a snowball at Carol, she throws one back
- Séance: say GOODBYE and the candles go out
- Fury: the jack-in, FIREWALL FURY in neon, ROUND 1, FIGHT!
- Slots: the AGI jackpot climbing (it has never hit)
- The Machine: `/override`, or something shocks Cora and the whole window tears

Swap-ins if one films badly: the ICQ "Uh-oh!" and Daisy's Online Alert, Cal calling you back on Skype, Arthur's reddit thread nesting until "continue this thread →", Grug's torch pointer finding handprints in the rock.

## Things to check

- **The site still mentions the hidden Gptys.** The Meet page's "Other agents can" table has Dale ("Browse the web") and the 1989 Shelly. Its anti-features mention Dale and Nick, and Nick's profile card is still in there. Anyone who watches the video and then visits the site will see names they can't find.
- **The site's costume count is sixteen, not twelve.** The Meet page counts every agent, hidden ones included, so it currently reads "One model. Sixteen costumes." The video says twelve (the sidebar). One of them needs to change before recording.
- **There's no `.kiro/` folder in the repo.** If the specs, steering and hooks live somewhere else, they won't show up in a shot of the repo. Beat 7 needs something on screen that shows Kiro doing work.
- **Kayleigh's AI joke.** If she doesn't land A1 on her own after a few tries, a one-line hint in her persona would make it reliable. That's a product change, so only if you want it.

## Open questions

1. **How much Kiro?** The draft gives Kiro one beat (20 seconds). Kiroween judging looks at how the project used Kiro, so you may want a longer beat here (specs, hooks, steering) at the cost of easter egg time.
2. **Who does the VO?** You, a text-to-speech voice, or Cora's voice? A flat synthetic voice would suit the "straight AI launch" tone.
3. **Music.** A generic upbeat AI-launch track that cuts out, and each era's sound takes over (Uh-oh!, the Skype ring, the slot machine bells)?
