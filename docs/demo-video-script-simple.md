# Demo video script: the simple version

About 1:30 and about 130 words of voiceover. Seven beats, one idea each. The Meet page is the source for the lines, so the video and the site say the same things.

For more shot ideas and spare jokes, see the longer plan in `docs/demo-video-script.md`.

| # | Time | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:12 | Every agent is smart |
| 2 | 0:12–0:20 | Meet the Gptys. They can't. |
| 3 | 0:20–0:35 | Personality, stuck in time |
| 4 | 0:35–0:50 | Built for the Costume Contest |
| 5 | 0:50–1:10 | What every Gpty comes with |
| 6 | 1:10–1:25 | The roll call |
| 7 | 1:25–1:35 | Try one |

---

## 1. Every agent is smart (0:00–0:12)

**VO**
> AI agents are getting smarter. Faster. Better. They browse the web, read your inbox, write your code and book your flights.

**On screen:** `gpty.lol/clean`, the plain modern chat. A tidy answer streams in. Upbeat, generic AI-launch music.

## 2. Meet the Gptys. They can't. (0:12–0:20)

**VO**
> Meet the Jippities. They can't do any of that.

**On screen:** the music cuts out. Hard cut to the Meet page hero: "AI agents that can't do anything", with the twelve Gptys' eyes following the pointer.

## 3. Personality, stuck in time (0:20–0:35)

**VO**
> Each one is a whole lot of personality, stuck in its own moment in time. Other AIs apologise for their knowledge cutoff. Jippities *are* their knowledge cutoff.

**On screen:** scroll slowly through "Other agents have features — ours have excuses". Let three rows read on their own:
- Use your computer → Grug has a rock
- Run 24/7 → Kayleigh: brb mum needs the computer
- Give a straight answer → Lucky says pull again

## 4. Built for the Costume Contest (0:35–0:50)

**VO**
> We built them for Kiroween's Costume Contest. Every Jippity is a real AI chat, dressed up as an app you used to love. Or one you never could have.

**On screen:** crossfade MSN → ICQ → Skype → Facebook. On "never could have", cut to the cave wall, then the séance board.

## 5. What every Gpty comes with (0:50–1:10)

**VO**
> Each one comes with its own costume, its own easter eggs, a real AI underneath, and an original theme song to match the vibe.

**On screen:** one shot per item, timed to the words:
- **Costume:** Grug's cave wall, the torch pointer finding handprints
- **Easter eggs:** a Nudge shakes the MSN window, then Kayleigh nudges back
- **Real AI:** Mortimer's planchette spelling out a real reply
- **Theme song:** the Now Playing player on Daisy, "Choosing Me" by Daisy Chain. Let the song come up under the VO.

## 6. The roll call (1:10–1:25)

**VO**
> Meet Daisy. She only cares about your A S L.
> Kayleigh's online after school, until her mum needs the computer.
> Cal can call you for free. For about seven seconds.
> Mark isn't that Mark. Please stop asking.
> Arthur has an answer for everything. He's kind of annoying like that.
> Channing killed email. He made a channel to celebrate.
> Grug has a rock. Grug very proud of rock.
> Mortimer doesn't hallucinate. He is the hallucination.
> Shelly is against you. She's fun.
> Carol ships only one night a year.
> Cora is AGI… I think?
> And Lucky's always got a good feeling about the next one.

**On screen:** click down the sidebar, one Gpty per name, so each window lands as its name is said. Each click swaps the theme song, so the soundtrack turns into a channel-flip through every era. Hold a beat longer on Lucky: the reels spin.

## 7. Try one (1:25–1:35)

**VO**
> Try a Jippity today at jippity dot lol, and talk nonsense with some useless agents.

**End card:** `gpty.lol` with the twelve Gptys lined up, eyes following the cursor. Under it, small:
- *Built with Kiro IDE and Claude.*
- *No agents were made useful in the making of this site.*

## Notes

- **Where it goes:** the Meet page has a "Demo video coming soon" slot under the hero. This version is short enough to autoplay there muted, with captions.
- **Kiro:** this cut drops the Kiro IDE beat and keeps Kiro to the end card. That suits the Costume Contest track. If the submission also needs to show how Kiro was used, add 10–15 seconds of the repo in Kiro between beats 6 and 7.
- **VO voice:** a flat, synthetic launch-video voice suits beats 1 and 2. It could warm up slightly from beat 3 onwards, once the Gptys take over.
