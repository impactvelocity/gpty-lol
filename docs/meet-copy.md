## The bit, in one line

Write it like a straight-faced AI agent launch page (big gradient hero, "Trusted by", a feature comparison, benchmarks, FAQ) where every feature is missing and the characters supply the punchlines.

Rules that keep it funny:

- **Deadpan voice.** The page sounds like it is selling. The jokes come from the facts: nudges, a/s/l, the mum who needs the phone line, Grug counting to ten. No "lol so random" winking.
- **One joke per line.** A short setup and a real era detail, then stop.
- **They're useless as agents, not as company.** The personas really do help with your question in their own voice (that's how they're prompted). So the joke is "can't do anything an agent does", and the twist at the end is "they'll still talk to you". That keeps the page from being one note, and it's true to the product.
- **Name the category, not the rivals.** "Other agents" / "Your agent" in the comparison. Real product names date fast, and the joke works without them. (If you want named targets, parody names like *Grokbot* or *Musebot* are safer than real ones.)

## Anti-features

Grid of cards, styled like a real feature grid (icon, bold title, one line). Pick 6 or 9.

- ★ **No computer use.** Grug has a rock. Mortimer has a planchette. Dale has a computer, but his mum's on the phone.
- ★ **No tools.** Kayleigh has one tool. It's `/nudge`.
- **No web access.** Nick is downloading the internet. 56k. 6 hours remaining.
- ★ **No integrations.** Kayleigh can't connect to your Google Drive. She can burn you a CD.
- **No MCP.** Nick has OP. Not the same thing.
- ★ **Knowledge cutoff: the whole personality.** Other AIs apologise for their knowledge cutoff. Gptys *are* their knowledge cutoff.
- **Context window: one lifetime.** Grug's is ten fingers.
- **Streaming, technically.** Mortimer answers one letter at a time.
- **Reasoning mode.** Grug has a `/think` mode. The answers get longer.
- **No hallucinations.** Except Mortimer, and he's real.
- **Not SOC 2 compliant.** Linda from Legal says everything is discoverable.
- **Multimodal.** Text, nudges, pokes, sheep, snowballs and a large trout.
- **No background tasks.** If someone picks up the phone, Daisy gets cut off.

## Built for Kiroween: the Costume Contest
